## 1. Private Markets Deployment Remains Broad-Based

Private market commitments dominated board agendas across all four weeks of July, spanning private equity, private credit, real estate, and infrastructure. **Nebraska Investment Council** (~$14B) approved **$130 million** across three funds — **$40 million** to **The Jordan Company Resolute Fund VII**, **$50 million** to **Ares Real Estate Secondaries X**, and **$40 million** to **Realterm Logistics Fund V**, each passing 4-0 (doc_id=5252). **TRSL** (~$25B) committed up to **$150 million** to **ICG North American Credit Partners Fund III** and up to **$200 million** to **Kayne Anderson Core Real Estate, L.P.**, with due-diligence support from **Hamilton Lane Advisors** and **StepStone Group** (doc_id=5164). **Metro Nashville ERS** (~$2B) approved **$95 million** across **LGT Crown Europe Small Buyouts VII** (**$20 million**), **Savano Capital Partners IV** (**$35 million**), and **O'Brien Staley Partners Value Add Fund V** (**$40 million**), all unanimous (doc_id=5257).

In the following week, **DCRB** (~$13B) ratified up to **$75 million** to FUND AS (private real estate) and up to **$125 million** to FUND AT (private equity), each approved **10-0**, and reported that a **$100 million** commitment to **Ares Pathfinder Fund III** closed May 29, 2026 (doc_id=5359). **SERS Ohio** (~$18B) approved up to **$50 million** to **Marathon Asset-Based Lending Fund IV** and up to **$50 million** to **Stepstone VC Secondaries VII** (doc_id=5280). **BRS** (~$7B) committed **$20 million** each to **Arcmont Direct Lending Fund V** and **Sound Point U.S. Direct Lending Fund IV**, both approved **5-0** as part of a **$115 million** annual private debt pacing plan (doc_id=5343). **SMCERA** (~$5B) executed a **$25 million** re-up to **LS Power Fund VI** via CEO delegated authority (doc_id=5317).

## 2. Performance Divergence: Strong Trailing Returns Mask Q1 2026 Softness

Several plans reported strong trailing twelve-month results alongside a softer Q1 2026. **TRS Texas** (~$200B) posted a Q1 2026 total fund return of **-0.3%** versus a policy benchmark of **-0.1%**, while the 1-year return through March 31, 2026 was **14.4%** versus **13.1%** and the 3-year return was **9.6%** versus **7.8%** (doc_id=5236). The trust grew from **$208.3B** to **$233.5B** over the trailing 1-year period, with **$29.8B** in net investment gains despite **$4.6B** in net cash outflows (doc_id=5236). Real Estate posted **-2.9%** for the 1-year period versus a **2.9%** NCREIF ODCE benchmark, while Commodities returned **104.2%** versus a **43.0%** benchmark (doc_id=5236).

**SMCERA** similarly reported a Q1 2026 total fund return of **-0.3%** versus a **0.1%** benchmark, with April 2026 returning **4.3%** (preliminary) and the trailing 12-month net return reaching **16.7%** through April 30, 2026 (doc_id=5317). **SERS Ohio** reported a 1-year net return of **18.86%** versus a **16.16%** policy benchmark and a 5-year net return of **9.06%** versus **7.43%**, with a total fund balance of **$23.43 billion** as of May 31, 2026 (doc_id=5219, doc_id=5280). **DCRB** reported a new high total market value of **$14.7 billion** as of July 6, 2026, with a calendar YTD net return of **7.5%** and a fiscal YTD 2026 return of **9.8%** (doc_id=5359). **SCERS Seattle** (~$4B) reported a FY2025 total fund return of **14.8%** net of fees versus a strategic policy benchmark of **14.1%**, with public equity returning **22.9%** (doc_id=5201).

*Sources: [TRS Texas — Board Pack — July 01, 2026](?doc=5236), [SMCERA — Board Pack — July 28, 2026](?doc=5317), [SERS Ohio — Board Pack — July 16, 2026](?doc=5219), [SERS Ohio — Board Pack — July 01, 2026](?doc=5280), [DCRB — Minutes — July 16, 2026](?doc=5359), [SCERS Seattle — Board Pack — December 31, 2025](?doc=5201)*

## 3. Asset Allocation Policy Updates and Manager Concentration Limits

July saw multiple plans formalizing strategic allocation changes. **TRS Texas** amended its Investment Policy Statement to increase the public markets portfolio manager concentration limit to **5%** of portfolio market value and the total Trust manager concentration limit to **8%** cumulative across portfolios, after two public markets managers passively exceeded existing limits due to strong performance; the board noted the limits had not been updated since 2008, and adopted the amendment unanimously (doc_id=5233, doc_id=5231). **SERS Ohio** adopted a new private credit benchmark presented by **Wilshire Associates** and approved a FY2027 Annual Investment Plan with targets of **40%** Global Equities, **18%** Global Fixed Income, **13%** Global Real Estate, **14%** Global Private Equity, **5%** Global Private Credit, and **7%** Global Infrastructure, effective July 1, 2026 (doc_id=5219).

**WSIB** (~$200B) is implementing Private Credit as a new standalone asset class in 2026 and funded a lifecycle active global equity strategy in Q2 2026, while conducting final due diligence on an active emerging markets equity strategy (doc_id=5275). **NCRS** (~$120B) executed multiple rebalances based on its new Strategic Asset Allocation, with the NCIA having previously approved a revised strategic asset allocation modestly increasing allocations to real assets including infrastructure and energy, with implementation to occur gradually to minimize transaction costs and market disruption (doc_id=5337). **SMCERA** approved an updated Asset Allocation Policy Portfolio following its Asset-Liability Study Part III, retaining a **6.25%** net investment return assumption for the June 30, 2026 valuation as recommended by **Milliman**; **Meketa**'s 10-year expected return is **6.4%** (6.2% net of expenses) (doc_id=5317).

*Sources: [TRS Texas — Board Pack — July 01, 2026](?doc=5233), [TRS Texas — Board Pack — July 01, 2026](?doc=5231), [SERS Ohio — Board Pack — July 16, 2026](?doc=5219), [WSIB — Board Pack — Date unknown](?doc=5275), [NCRS — Minutes — April 30, 2026](?doc=5337), [SMCERA — Board Pack — July 28, 2026](?doc=5317)*

**Practical implication:** The simultaneous policy updates at **TRS Texas**, **SERS Ohio**, **WSIB**, and **SMCERA** indicate a broad mid-cycle recalibration of allocation frameworks; managers pitching private credit or real assets mandates will find receptive audiences at plans actively formalizing these exposures.

## 4. Leadership Transitions and Governance Actions

CIO-level turnover and consulting contract decisions were a recurring theme. **ERS-HI** (~$23B) appointed Deputy CIO **Anthony Goo** as interim CIO effective June 1, 2026, following the departure of CIO **Kristin Varela**, with a national executive search expected to conclude by end of 2026 or early 2027 (doc_id=5187). **NVPERS** (~$55B) unanimously approved an Investment Consulting Services Agreement with **Callan** and appointed **Steve Edmundson** as new Executive Officer effective July 7, 2026 (doc_id=5251), and subsequently confirmed **Lauren Larson** as Chief Investment Officer effective July 16, 2026 (doc_id=5315). **SERS Ohio** retained **CBIZ** as compensation consultant, selected from six vendor proposals including **Aon MacLagan**, **Gallagher**, **MGT**, **Moore Cooperative**, and **MorganHR**, with a contract effective August 1, 2026; Executive Director **Richard Stensrud** is retiring, employed through July 31, 2026 for transition at **$30,519.20** (doc_id=5220, doc_id=5219).

On proxy and ESG governance, **Nebraska Investment Council** adopted a revised Proxy Voting Policy directing **Glass Lewis** to vote against DEI and ESG proposals and for proposals to eliminate such policies, passing 4-0 (doc_id=5252). **TRS Texas** has a December 2026 board agenda item for selection of a proxy voting advisor, with a Proxy Policy review and Proxy Advisor RFP targeted for completion March 2027 (doc_id=5231). **LACERS** (~$28.25 billion as of June 22, 2026) approved a key person replacement for **Aksia LLC** in its private equity consultant role **7-0** (doc_id=5342).

## 5. Manager Searches, Fee Negotiations, and Actuarial Assumptions

Active manager selection and fee discipline surfaced across multiple plans. **LACERS** has a pending vote on a 3-year contract renewal with **Wellington Management Company LLP** for an active emerging market debt portfolio valued at **$581 million**, at a fee of **47 bps** (~**$2.7M** annually), with a fee concession secured from the manager and NEPC concurring with the renewal; the portfolio returned **14.27%** versus a **12.16%** benchmark for the 1-year period ending May 31, 2026, benchmarked against a **50% JPM EMBI Global Diversified / 50% JPM GBI-EM Global Diversified** custom benchmark (doc_id=5342). **BRS** retained **RhumbLine**'s S&P 500 Pooled Index Fund as incumbent manager in the Large Cap Core space **5-0**, following a search that included analysis of **D.E. Shaw** (doc_id=5343). **LACERS** received **33 responses** to its Infrastructure RFP (6 passive, 27 active mandates), with award pending (doc_id=5342).

On actuarial assumptions, **CT SERS** (~$20B) received a preliminary five-year Experience Study from **CavMac** actuaries recommending retention of the **6.9%** investment return assumption, with salary growth changes estimated to produce a **$546M** increase in unfunded liability and an **$84.6M** increase in ADEC; formal approval was deferred (doc_id=5358). **SMCERA** approved demographic assumption changes from **Milliman**'s 2026 Experience Study estimated to increase the employer Statutory Contribution Rate by **0.28%** and slightly increase the funded ratio by **0.20%** (doc_id=5317). **SCERS Seattle** reported its GASB 67 funded ratio improved to **83.54%** as of December 31, 2025, from **75.99%** in 2024 (doc_id=5201).
