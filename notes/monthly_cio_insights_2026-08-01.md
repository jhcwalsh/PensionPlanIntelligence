**NHRS** (~$9B) achieved a preliminary net return of **11.83%** for FY2026, with plan assets of **$14.7 billion** (doc_id=5698). **VRS** (~$110B) reported its Exposure Management Portfolio returned **26.0%** versus a **15.1%** benchmark on a one-year basis as of May 31, 2026, with total fund 20-year forward return expectations of **7.2–7.3%**, above the **6.75%** actuarial target (doc_id=5580).

At its July 28 meeting, LACERS also approved a three-year renewal with **Wellington Management Company LLP** for a **$581 million** emerging market debt mandate (7-0), effective December 1, 2026 through November 30, 2029 (doc_id=5767). ## 4. Asset Allocation Shifts: De-Risking and Liability-Hedging

At least two plans executed or advanced formal asset allocation changes during August, moving toward reduced return-seeking exposure and greater liability alignment.

**SMCERA** adopted "Policy X," reducing return-seeking assets by **10%** and increasing liability-hedging assets by **10%** as part of an Asset Liability Study (6-0), with staff actively rebalancing by selling equity exposure to reduce an equity overweight relative to policy (doc_id=5752). An Asset Allocation Implementation Plan and Public Equity Manager Structure Proposal remained pending board approval at the August 25 meeting (doc_id=5753).

**VRS** shifted its Diversifying Strategies program target portfolio to **25% Risk Responders / 75% Return Enhancers**, with a new benchmark effective July 1, 2026 of **25% HFRI Institutional Trend Following Index / 75% ICE BAML 3-month Treasury Bill plus 250 bps** (doc_id=5580). The fund's leverage program, operational since January 2024, stood at approximately **0.5%** of fund as of Q3 2026, with untapped maximum leverage capacity of **$6.354 billion** (doc_id=5580).

**ERS-GA** retired its Target Maturity Bond Fund in early July 2026, migrating remaining assets to a Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities fund (doc_id=5747). **DPFP** presented an Asset Allocation Study using **BlackRock** capital market assumptions, including 10-year net expected returns of **11.58%** for Private Equity, **9.85%** for Infrastructure, **7.92%** for Private Credit Direct Lending, and **7.89%** for US Large Cap (doc_id=5645).
