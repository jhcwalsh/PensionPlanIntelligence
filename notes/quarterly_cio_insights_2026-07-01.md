# Insights: Q3 2026 in Review
*Synthesized from 2 approved monthly briefings (2026-07-01 – 2026-09-30)*
*Generated: October 13, 2026*

---

Private markets deployment dominated board agendas in both months of the quarter, spanning buyout, private credit, real estate, infrastructure, and venture across plans ranging from ~$2B to ~$200B. Performance reporting shifted decisively over the quarter — July briefings paired strong trailing-twelve-month results with Q1 2026 softness, while August delivered broadly strong fiscal-year 2026 outcomes with meaningful dispersion. Allocation policy work evolved in parallel, from July's mid-cycle recalibration of frameworks to August's explicit de-risking and liability-hedging moves.

## 1. Private Markets Deployment Remains the Quarter's Dominant Activity

July commitments were broad-based across all four weeks. **Nebraska Investment Council** (~$14B) approved **$130 million** across three funds — **$40 million** to **The Jordan Company Resolute Fund VII**, **$50 million** to **Ares Real Estate Secondaries X**, and **$40 million** to **Realterm Logistics Fund V**, each passing 4-0 (doc_id=5252). **TRSL** (~$25B) committed up to **$150 million** to **ICG North American Credit Partners Fund III** and up to **$200 million** to **Kayne Anderson Core Real Estate, L.P.** (doc_id=5164). **DCRB** (~$13B) ratified up to **$75 million** to FUND AS and up to **$125 million** to FUND AT, each approved **10-0** (doc_id=5359), while **BRS** (~$7B) committed **$20 million** each to **Arcmont Direct Lending Fund V** and **Sound Point U.S. Direct Lending Fund IV**, both **5-0**, as part of a **$115 million** annual private debt pacing plan (doc_id=5343).

August sustained the pace at larger scale. **LACERA** (~$75B) approved **$220 million** to **Kingswood Capital Opportunities Fund IV** and **$100 million** to **Kingswood Capital Opportunities Small Cap Fund I** (doc_id=5499). **MainePERS** (~$18B) ratified up to **$300 million** to **Brookfield Super-Core Infrastructure Partners** (7-1, Fuller Googins abstaining), up to **$250 million** to **WhiteHawk V Onshore Fund** (8-0), and up to **$75 million** to **GTCR Fund XV** (8-0) (doc_id=5581). **SJCERA** (~$2B) reported a **$75 million** commitment to **Hines Rialto Credit Partners Fund** on a **6-0** vote (doc_id=5522), and **KPPA** (~$20B) previously approved up to **$100 million** to the **ITE Rail Fund** (doc_id=5739).

Deployment ran alongside repositioning: **MainePERS** simultaneously authorized a full redemption of its **King Street Capital** strategy (8-0) (doc_id=5581). **SMCERA** (~$5B) appeared in both months, executing a **$25 million** re-up to **LS Power Fund VI** via CEO delegated authority in July (doc_id=5317) and a **$30 million** re-up to **Endeavour Capital Fund IX L.P.** plus a **$15 million** aggregate commitment to two **Bessemer Venture Partners** vehicles in August, both 6-0 (doc_id=5752).

## 2. Performance: Q1 Softness Gives Way to Strong Fiscal-Year Results

July's reporting showed a split picture. **TRS Texas** (~$200B) posted a Q1 2026 total fund return of **-0.3%** versus a policy benchmark of **-0.1%**, while the 1-year return through March 31, 2026 was **14.4%** versus **13.1%** (doc_id=5236). **SMCERA** reported the same Q1 2026 total fund return of **-0.3%** versus a **0.1%** benchmark, with April 2026 returning **4.3%** (preliminary) and a trailing 12-month net return of **16.7%** through April 30, 2026 (doc_id=5317). **SERS Ohio** (~$18B) reported a 1-year net return of **18.86%** versus a **16.16%** policy benchmark (doc_id=5219), and **DCRB** reported a new high total market value of **$14.7 billion** as of July 6, 2026 with a fiscal YTD 2026 return of **9.8%** (doc_id=5359).

August confirmed the recovery thesis flagged in July. **ERS-GA** (~$20B) reported an approximate **16.5%** rate of return for FY2026, with total fund market value growing from **$18.584 billion** at June 30, 2025 to **$21.369 billion** at June 30, 2026 (doc_id=5747). **MN TRA** (~$30B) reported approximately **+16%** for FY2026, improving its market-value funded ratio from **84.5%** to **92.2%** (doc_id=5745). **MainePERS** posted a trailing 1-year total fund return of **8.2%** versus a **3.6%** benchmark, with Private Equity leading at **37.0%** versus a **16.3%** benchmark (doc_id=5581). **NHRS** (~$9B) achieved a preliminary net return of **11.83%** for FY2026 (doc_id=5698). **SMCERA**'s trailing twelve-month figure moderated to a preliminary **14.2%** through June 2026 (doc_id=5752), from the **16.7%** reported through April (doc_id=5317).

The dispersion running counter to the trend sat at **LACERA**, which reported a total fund 1-year net return of **12.2%** versus a **14.8%** policy benchmark as of June 2026, with Growth lagging at **17.5%** versus **23.3%** (doc_id=5499).

## 3. Allocation Policy: From Mid-Cycle Recalibration to Explicit De-Risking

July's allocation work was framework-level. **TRS Texas** amended its Investment Policy Statement to increase the public markets portfolio manager concentration limit to **5%** of portfolio market value and the total Trust manager concentration limit to **8%** cumulative, after two public markets managers passively exceeded existing limits due to strong performance (doc_id=5233). **SERS Ohio** approved a FY2027 Annual Investment Plan with targets of **40%** Global Equities, **18%** Global Fixed Income, **13%** Global Real Estate, **14%** Global Private Equity, **5%** Global Private Credit, and **7%** Global Infrastructure (doc_id=5219). **WSIB** (~$200B) is implementing Private Credit as a new standalone asset class in 2026 (doc_id=5275), and **NCRS** (~$120B) executed multiple rebalances based on its new Strategic Asset Allocation (doc_id=5337).

August turned directional. **SMCERA**, which in July approved an updated Asset Allocation Policy Portfolio following its Asset-Liability Study Part III and retained a **6.25%** net investment return assumption (doc_id=5317), adopted "Policy X" in August — reducing return-seeking assets by **10%** and increasing liability-hedging assets by **10%** (6-0), with staff actively selling equity exposure to reduce an equity overweight (doc_id=5752). **VRS** (~$110B) shifted its Diversifying Strategies program target portfolio to **25% Risk Responders / 75% Return Enhancers**, with a new benchmark effective July 1, 2026 (doc_id=5580). **ERS-GA** retired its Target Maturity Bond Fund in early July 2026, migrating remaining assets to a Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities fund (doc_id=5747).

## 4. Manager Turnover and Fee Discipline Intensified

The quarter's clearest single thread ran through **LACERS** (~$28.25 billion as of June 22, 2026). In July, a 3-year renewal with **Wellington Management Company LLP** for a **$581 million** active emerging market debt portfolio was pending, at a fee of **47 bps** (~**$2.7M** annually), with a fee concession secured and the portfolio returning **14.27%** versus a **12.16%** benchmark for the 1-year period ending May 31, 2026 (doc_id=5342). August confirmed the outcome: the **Wellington** renewal was approved **7-0**, effective December 1, 2026 through November 30, 2029 (doc_id=5767). LACERS also renewed **PGIM, Inc.** for active emerging market blended debt on a **$602 million** allocation at **33 basis points** annually, ranking in the **11th percentile** versus peers in the eVestment database (doc_id=5767), and notified the board of expiring contracts for **Loomis, Sayles & Company, L.P.**, **Polen Capital Credit, LLC**, and **Barrow, Hanley, Mewhinney & Strauss, LLC** (doc_id=5503).

Competitive incumbent reviews featured in both months. **BRS** retained **RhumbLine**'s S&P 500 Pooled Index Fund as incumbent manager in Large Cap Core **5-0**, following a search that included analysis of **D.E. Shaw** (doc_id=5343). **LACERS** received **33 responses** to its Infrastructure RFP in July (doc_id=5342). In August, **DPFP** (~$3B) unanimously terminated **Manulife Global Equity** and approved agreements with **Arrowstreet** for up to a **6%** global equity allocation (doc_id=5645); **KPPA** transferred the MSCI ACWI Ex-US mandate from **Franklin Templeton** to **Clearbridge** (doc_id=5739); and **LAFPP** (~$29B) approved a three-year extension with **Brandes Investment Partners, L.P.** by a **9-0** vote (doc_id=5505). **KPPA** reported investment fees declined **4%** year-over-year despite a **13%** increase in total assets, with staff negotiating **12 of 14** managers into the lowest fee quartile, saving an estimated **$500,000** per year (doc_id=5739).
